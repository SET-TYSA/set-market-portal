01 / Our heritage

Built on commercial experience

SETFOODS brings together commercial experience, knowledge of agricultural markets and a long-term approach to international business. Based in Chile, we connect producers, exporters, importers, distributors and industrial buyers through a diversified sourcing network.

Our approach combines traditional business values with market intelligence, procurement and supply chain coordination. We focus on transparency, reliable communication and enduring commercial relationships.

03 / Business model

Independent brokerage & global sourcing

Commercial services

International brokerage, supplier identification, procurement, market development, commercial negotiation, export coordination and logistics support.

International reach

South American sourcing with international market development across the Americas, Europe, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East, subject to individual commercial arrangements.

SETFOODS primarily acts as an independent commercial intermediary. Transaction responsibilities, commissions and payment terms are agreed on a case-by-case basis.

04 / Quality & due diligence

Responsible commercial relationships

We promote transparent sourcing and appropriate counterparty verification. Depending on the transaction, documentation may include certificates of analysis, technical specifications, certificates of origin, phytosanitary and health certificates, food safety and organic certifications, traceability and customs documentation. All claims are subject to supplier and shipment verification.

We support responsible business practices, anti-bribery principles and applicable sanctions and regulatory compliance.
